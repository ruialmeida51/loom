# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

- The news list loads more stories as you scroll, and a page that fails to load offers Try again without hiding what you were reading.

## [4.0.20] - 2026-11-26

- Trips abroad: enter an expense in the currency you paid in, converted at today's rate (or yours).
- Long Money histories load faster, and balances stay right however many expenses you add.
- News stories show their pictures, and Today’s reads now sit right under your balance on Home.
- Photos no longer flicker back to initials when you switch tabs.

## [4.0.19] - 2026-11-26

- Share your MB Way, Revolut or PayPal so housemates can pay you back: MB Way copies your number and opens the app.
- Split an expense right on the form: tap people in or out and see each share.
- Money accounts can have icons, and payers show their photo in the ledger.
- Tidier list and account headers, a readable disabled Save in dark mode, and the photo pencil now works.

## [4.0.18] - 2026-11-26

- Guests on trips and events: people outside the household can pay, share costs and settle up there.
- Recurring bills are easier to find and set up, and new expenses show which account they go to.
- Assign chores, and let repeating ones take turns: Mine shows just yours.

## [4.0.17] - 2026-11-25

- Member photos on expense details, balances, the split sheet and Home.
- A clearer groceries card on Home with the list's name, count and next items.
- Lighter photos that load faster.

## [4.0.16] - 2026-11-25
