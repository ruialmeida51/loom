# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

## [4.0.5] - 2026-11-17

- Tasks replaces reminders: optional dates/alerts and a dedicated tab beside Money.
- Smooth loading and refresh skeletons while retaining drafts, focus and scroll.
- Recover household reads after session or connection interruptions.
- Compact dated expenses, current balances and simpler completed-grocery controls.
- Manage feeds, retry updates, edit addresses and remove sources; support Expresso.
- Edge-to-edge Home news, illustrated empty states and clearer settings/privacy buttons.

## [4.0.4] - 2026-11-16

- Capture expenses sooner with amount-first entry, compact summaries and smaller split controls.
- Keep loaded content visible during refresh and remove forced loading waits.
- Load Home sections independently, with compact shortcuts and a secondary news preview.
- Start reading with optional, editable BBC World and NASA feed suggestions.
- Apply a complete light/dark Material palette adapted from the original Lumbridge.

## [4.0.3] - 2026-11-16

- Select and delete completed groceries; keep checklist rows aligned while typing.
- Pull to refresh with skeletons and clearer reminder completion feedback.
- See upcoming reminders and a shopping preview on Home.
- Adjust split shares with plus/minus controls and scan clearer expense cards.
- Share six-character household codes; improved account spacing and selectors.

## [4.0.2] - 2026-11-16

- Keep typing shopping items with Enter, stable ordering and keyboard clearance.
- Delete shopping lists and trip accounts; manage archived trips separately.
- Edit expense splits in a sheet without losing your draft.
- Smoother startup, Back navigation, selectors and loading states.
- Correct adaptive and themed icons; Firebase builds reach internal testers.

## [4.0.1] - 2026-11-15

- Home now has square shortcuts for expenses and reminders.
- Find all reminders, Feed and household details under More.
- Keep everyday spending and trips in separate balance accounts.
- Split by shares, exact amounts or percentages, then record repayments.
- Edit expenses and manage recurring bills for each account.
- Switch households and manage your profile from the top bar.

## [4.0.0] - 2026-11-14

- Share shopping lists, reminders, expenses and monthly recurring bills.
- Choose a nickname, manage household invitations and switch homes.
- Keep household setup in place when returning to the app.
- Improve toolbar insets, editing sheets and back navigation.
